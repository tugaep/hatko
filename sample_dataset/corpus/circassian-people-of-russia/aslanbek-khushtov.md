# Aslanbek Khushtov

Aslanbek Vitalievich Khushtov (Russian: Асланбек Витальевич Хуштов; born 1 July 1980 in Beloglinka, Kabardino-Balkarian ASSR) is a Russian wrestler, who has won a gold medal in the 2008 Summer Olympics. Khushtov is a member of the Parliament of Kabardino-Balkarian Republic of the 4th convocation.

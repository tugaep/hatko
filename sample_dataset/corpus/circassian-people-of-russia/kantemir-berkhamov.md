# Kantemir Berkhamov

Kantemir Mikhailovich Berkhamov (Russian: Кантемир Михайлович Берхамов; born 7 August 1988) is a Russian former footballer who played as a central midfielder.

## Club career
He made his debut in the Russian Premier League on 22 March 2009 for PFC Spartak Nalchik in a game against FC Rubin Kazan.

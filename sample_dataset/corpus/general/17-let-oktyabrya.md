# 17 let Oktyabrya

17 let Oktyabrya (Russian: 17 лет Октября; Adyghe: Октябрэм ия 17 илъэс, Oktjabrəm ija 17 itləs) is a rural locality (a village) in Kirovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 191 as of 2018.

## Streets
Pryamaya

## Geography
17 let Oktyabrya is located 18 km north of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Mafekhabl is the nearest rural locality.

# Sadovoye, Republic of Adygea

Sadovoye (Russian: Садовое; Adyghe: Чъыгхат) is a rural locality (a selo) and the administrative center of Sadovskoye Rural Settlement of Krasnogvardeysky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 1422 as of 2018. There are 15 streets.

## Geography
Sadovoye is located 20 km southeast of Krasnogvardeyskoye (the district's administrative centre) by road. Bzhedugkhabl is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The village is inhabited by Russians (49.4%) and Kurds (46.6%) according to the 2010 census.

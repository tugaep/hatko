# Tauykhabl

Tauykhabl (Russian: Тауйхабль; Adyghe: Тэуехьабл) is a rural locality (an aul) in Dzhidzhikhablskoye Rural Settlement of Teuchezhsky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 197 as of 2018. There are 5 streets.

## Geography
Tauykhabl is located 14 km north of Ponezhukay (the district's administrative centre) by road. Dzhidzhikhabl is the nearest rural locality.

# Kabekhabl

Kabekhabl (Russian: Кабехабль; Adyghe: Къэбэхьабл) is a rural locality (an aul) in Khatazhukayskoye Rural Settlement of Shovgenovsky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 1,015 as of 2018. There are 14 streets.

## Geography
Kabekhabl is in the steppe zone, on the left bank of the Fars River, 8 km (5.0 mi) northwest of Khakurinokhabl (the district's administrative centre) by road. Pshicho is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Circassians.

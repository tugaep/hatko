# Obraztsovoye

Obraztsovoye (Russian: Образцовое; Adyghe: Образцовэр) is a rural locality (a selo) in Ayryumovskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 361 as of 2018. There are 3 streets.

## Geography
The village is located on the Ayrym River, 13 km east of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Progress is the nearest rural locality.

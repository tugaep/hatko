# Pustosyolov

Pustosyolov (Russian: Пустосёлов; Adyghe: ЧылэнэкI) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Yelenovskoye Rural Settlement of Koshekhablsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 8 in 2018. There are four streets.

## Geography
Pustosyolov is located 16 km east of Krasnogvardeyskoye (the district's administrative centre) by road. Nekrasovskaya is the nearest rural locality.

# Preobrazhenskoye, Republic of Adygea

Preobrazhenskoye (Russian: Преображенское; Adyghe: Цуукӏэй) is a rural locality (a selo) in Beloselskoye Rural Settlement of Krasnogvardeysky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 1469 as of 2018. There are 19 streets.

## Geography
Preobrazhenskoye is located 10 km south of Krasnogvardeyskoye (the district's administrative centre) by road. Beloye is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The village is inhabited by  Kurds (66.2%) and Russians (31.1%) according to the 2010 census.

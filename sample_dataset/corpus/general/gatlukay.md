# Gatlukay

Gatlukay (Russian: Гатлукай; Adyghe: Хьэлъэкъуай) is a rural locality (an aul) in Adygeysk District of Adygea, Russia. The population was 1555 as of 2018. There are 15 streets.

## Geography
Gatlukay is located 6 km east of Adygeysk (the district's administrative centre) by road. Pchegatlukay is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Circassians.

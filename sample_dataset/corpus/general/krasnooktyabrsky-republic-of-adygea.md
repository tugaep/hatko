# Krasnooktyabrsky, Republic of Adygea

Krasnooktyabrsky (Russian: Краснооктябрьский; Adyghe: Октябрэплъыжь, Oktjabrəptlyź) is a rural locality (a settlement) and the administrative center of Krasnooktyabrskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 6536 as of 2018. There are 69 streets.

## Geography
Krasnooktyabrsky is located 14 km northwest of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Prirechny is the nearest rural locality.

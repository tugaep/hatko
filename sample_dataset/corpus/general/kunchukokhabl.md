# Kunchukokhabl

Kunchukokhabl (Russian: Кунчукохабль; Adyghe: Къунчыкъохьабл) is a rural locality (an aul) in Dzhidzhikhablskoye Rural Settlement of Teuchezhsky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 519 as of 2018. There are 10 streets.

## Geography
The aul is on the shore of the Gulf of Krasnodar reservoir, 19 km northeast of Ponezhukay (the district's administrative centre) by road. Gorodskoy is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Circassians.

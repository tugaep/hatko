# Nizhniy Ayryum

Nizhniy Ayryum (Russian: Нижний Айрюм; Adyghe: Ычӏэгъ Арым) is a rural locality (a selo) in Ayryumovskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 264 as of 2018. There are 4 streets.

## Geography
Nizhniy Ayryum is located 16 km east of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Novy and Obraztsovoye are the nearest rural localities.

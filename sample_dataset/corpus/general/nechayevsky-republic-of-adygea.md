# Nechayevsky, Republic of Adygea

Nechayevsky (Russian: Нечаевский; Adyghe: Нечаевскэр) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Dondukovskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 25 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography
Nechayevsky is located 31 km east of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Smolchev-Malinovsky is the nearest rural locality.

# Abadzekhskaya

Abadzekhskaya (Russian: Абадзехская, Adyghe: Абдзах, romanized: Abdzax) is a rural locality (a stanitsa) in Maykopsky District of the Republic of Adygea, Russia, located on the Belaya River 28 kilometers (17 mi) south of Maykop. Population: 3,623 (2010 census); 4,008 (2002 census).

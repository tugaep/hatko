# Khanskaya

Khanskaya (Russian: Ха́нская; Adyghe: Хъанхьабл) is a rural locality (a stanitsa) under the administrative jurisdiction of Maykop Republican Urban Okrug in the Republic of Adygea, Russia, located on the Belaya River 13 kilometers (8.1 mi) northwest of Maykop. The population as of 2020 is 11,679.
It was established in 1862.

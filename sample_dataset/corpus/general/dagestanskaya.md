# Dagestanskaya

Dagestanskaya (Russian: Дагестанская; Adyghe: Дэгъыстан, Dəġystan) is a rural locality (a stanitsa) in Krasnooktyabrskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 495 as of 2018. There are 19 streets.

## Geography
The stanitsa is located on the Kurdzhips River, 34 km southwest of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Krasny Dagestan is the nearest rural locality.

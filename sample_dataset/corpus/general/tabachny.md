# Tabachny

Tabachny (Russian: Табачный; Adyghe: ТутыншӏапI) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Krasnooktyabrskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 2008 as of 2018. There are 52 streets.

## Geography
Tabachny is located 16 km northwest of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Sadovy is the nearest rural locality.

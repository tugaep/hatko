# Dneprovsky

Dneprovsky (Russian: Днепровский; Adyghe: Днепровскэр) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Sergiyevskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 175 as of 2018. There are 3 streets.

## Geography
The khutor is located on the right bank of the Gachucha River, 40 km southeast of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Kolkhozny is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The khutor is inhabited by Russians.

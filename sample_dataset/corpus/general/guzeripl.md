# Guzeripl

Guzeripl (Russian: Гузерипль; Adyghe: Гъузэрыплъ, Ġuzəryptl) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Dakhovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 103 as of 2018. There are 5 streets.

## Geography
The settlement is located on the left bank of the Belaya River, 72 km south of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Khamyshki is the nearest rural locality.

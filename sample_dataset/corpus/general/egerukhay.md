# Egerukhay

Egerukhay (Russian: Егерухай; Adyghe: Еджэрыкъуай) is a rural locality (an aul) in Egerukhayskoye Rural Settlement of Koshekhablsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population of this aul was 1,635 as of 2018. There are 21 streets.

## Geography
The aul is located on the left bank of the Laba River, 20 km northwest of Koshekhabl (the district's administrative centre) by road. Luchezarny is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Adyghes or Circassians.

# Gaverdovsky

Gaverdovsky (Russian: Гавердовский, romanized: Gaverdovskiy; Adyghe: Гавердовск, romanized: Gaverdovsk) is a khutor in Koshekhablsky District, urban okrug of Maykop, Russia. The population was 3,824 as of 2018. There are 65 streets.

## Geography
The khutor is located on the right bank of the Belaya River, 8 km west of Maykop (the district's administrative centre) by road. Maykop is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The khutor is inhabited by Circassians, Pontic and Tsalka Greeks and Russians.

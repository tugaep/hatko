# Saratovsky, Republic of Adygea

Saratovsky (Russian: Саратовский; Adyghe: Байкъан) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Yelenovskoye Rural Settlement of Koshekhablsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 480 as of 2018. There are 30 streets.

## Geography
Saratovsky is located 16 km southeast of Krasnogvardeyskoye (the district's administrative centre) by road. Yelenovskoye is the nearest rural locality.

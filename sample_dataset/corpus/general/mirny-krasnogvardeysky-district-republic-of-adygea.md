# Mirny, Krasnogvardeysky District, Republic of Adygea

Mirny (Russian: Мирный; Adyghe: Мамырныгъ) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Beloselskoye Rural Settlement of Krasnogvardeysky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 10 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography
Mirny is located 12 km south of Krasnogvardeyskoye (the district's administrative centre) by road. Papenkov is the nearest rural locality.

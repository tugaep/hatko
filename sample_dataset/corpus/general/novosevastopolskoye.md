# Novosevastopolskoye

Novosevastopolskoye (Russian: Новосевастопольское; Adyghe: СевастополыкI) is a rural locality (a selo) in Beloselskoye Rural Settlement of Krasnogvardeysky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 834 as of 2018. There are 15 streets.

## Geography
Novosevastopolskoye is located 18 km southeast of Krasnogvardeyskoye (the district's administrative centre) by road. Bogursukov is the nearest rural locality.

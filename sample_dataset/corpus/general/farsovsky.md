# Farsovsky

Farsovsky (Russian: Фарсовский; Adyghe: Фарз) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Sergiyevskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population of this village was 11 as of 2018.

## Geography
The khutor is located on the right bank of the Fars River, 42 km southeast of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Tambovsky is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The khutor is inhabited by Russians.

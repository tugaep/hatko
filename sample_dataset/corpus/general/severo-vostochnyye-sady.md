# Severo-Vostochnyye Sady

Severo-Vostochnyye Sady (Russian: Северо-Восточные Сады; Adyghe: Темыр-Къокӏыпӏэ Чъыгхатэхэр) is a rural locality (a khutor) and the administrative center of Kirovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 3582 as of 2018. There are 40 streets.

## Geography
Severo-Vostochnyye Sady is located 15 km north of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Severny is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The khutor is inhabited by Russians, Armenians, Adygheans, Ukrainians and Kurds.

# Panakhes

Panakhes (Russian: Панахес; Adyghe: Пэнэхэс) is a rural locality (an aul) in Afipsipskoye Rural Settlement of Takhtamukaysky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 1,594 as of 2018. There are 35 streets.

## Geography
Panakhes is located 40 km northwest of Takhtamukay (the district's administrative centre) by road. Porono-Pokrovsky is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Circassians.

# Proletarsky, Republic of Adygea

Proletarsky (Russian: Пролетарский; Adyghe: Ермэл) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Kirovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 1064 as of 2018. There are 10 streets.

## Geography
Proletarsky is located 17 km north of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Mafekhabl is the nearest rural locality.

# Khamyshki

Khamyshki (Russian: Хамышки; Adyghe: Хъымыщкӏэй) is a rural locality (a selo) in Dakhovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 297 as of 2018. There are 22 streets.

## Geography
The village is located in the valley of the Belaya River, 57 km south of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Dakhovskaya is the nearest rural locality.

# Goncharka

Goncharka (Russian: Гончарка; Adyghe: Гончаркэр) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Giaginskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 1,472 as of 2018. There are 21 streets.

## Geography
Goncharka is located 16 km southwest of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Podgorny is the nearest rural locality.

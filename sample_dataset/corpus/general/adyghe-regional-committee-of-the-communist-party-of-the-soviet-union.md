# Adyghe Regional Committee of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union

The First Secretary of the Adyghe regional branch of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union (Adyghe: Совет Зэготым и Коммунист Партием и Адыгэ хэку комитет) was the position of highest authority in the Cherkess (Adyghe) AO (1922), Adyghe (Cherkess) AO (1922–1928), Adyghe AO (1928–1991) and the Adyghe ASSR (1991) in the Russian SFSR of the Soviet Union. The position was created on September 23, 1922, and abolished in August 1991. The First Secretary was a de facto appointed position usually by the Politburo or the General Secretary himself.

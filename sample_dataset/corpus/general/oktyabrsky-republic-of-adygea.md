# Oktyabrsky, Republic of Adygea

Oktyabrsky (Russian: Октябрьский; Adyghe: Чъэпогъу) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Kirovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 52 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography
Oktyabrsky is located 21 km north of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Grozny is the nearest rural locality.

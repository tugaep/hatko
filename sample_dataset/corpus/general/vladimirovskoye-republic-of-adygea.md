# Vladimirovskoye, Republic of Adygea

Vladimirovskoye (Russian: Владимировское; Adyghe: Владимировскэр) is a rural locality (a selo) in Kelermesskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 88 as of 2018. There are 4 streets.

## Geography
Vladimirovskoye is located 19 km southeast of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Tkachyov is the nearest rural locality.

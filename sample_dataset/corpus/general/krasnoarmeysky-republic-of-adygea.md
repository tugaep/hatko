# Krasnoarmeysky, Republic of Adygea

Krasnoarmeysky (Russian: Красноармейский; Adyghe: Дзэплъыжь) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Shendzhyskoye Rural Settlement of Takhtamukaysky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 46 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography
Krasnoarmeysky is located 14 km southeast of Takhtamukay (the district's administrative centre) by road. Novomogilyovsky is the nearest rural locality.

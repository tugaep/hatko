# Krasny Khleborob

Krasny Khleborob (Russian: Красный Хлебороб; Adyghe: Хьалыгъулэжь Плъыжь) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Ayryumovskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 43 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography
Krasny Khleborob is located 17 km east of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Progress is the nearest rural locality.

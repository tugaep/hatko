# Necherezy

Necherezy (Russian: Нечерезий; Adyghe: Нэчэрэзый) is a rural locality (an aul) in Ponezhukayskoye Rural Settlement of Teuchezhsky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 307 as of 2018. There are 3 streets.

## Geography
The aul is located in 5 km to the north of Ponezhukay aul.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Circassians.

# Neshukay

Neshukay (Russian: Нешукай; Adyghe: Нэшъукъуай) is a rural locality (an aul) in Ponezhukayskoye Rural Settlement of Teuchezhsky District, the Republic of Adygea, Russia. The population was 911 as of 2018. There are 14 streets.

## Geography
The aul is on the left bank of the Marta River, 4 km northeast of Ponezhukay (the district's administrative centre) by road. Ponezhukay is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The aul is inhabited by Russians and Adyghes.

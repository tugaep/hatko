# Komsomolsky, Republic of Adygea

Komsomolsky (Russian: Комсомольский; Adyghe: Комсомольскэр) is a rural locality (a settlement) in Mayskoye Rural Settlement of Koshekhablsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 159 as of 2018.

## Geography
Komsomolsky is located 9 km west of Koshekhabl (the district's administrative centre) by road. Nechayevsky is the nearest rural locality.

# Dmitriyevsky, Republic of Adygea

Dmitriyevsky (Russian: Дмитриевский; Adyghe: Дмитриевскэр) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Dmitriyevskoye Rural Settlement of Koshekhablsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population of this khutor was 479 as of 2018. There are 9 streets.

## Geography
Dmitriyevsky is located 14 km northwest of Koshekhabl (the district's administrative centre) by road. Druzhba is the nearest rural locality.

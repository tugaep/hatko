# Grazhdansky, Republic of Adygea

Grazhdansky (Russian: Гражданский; Adyghe: Гражданскэр, Graždanskər) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Krasnoulskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 379 as of 2018. There are three streets.

## Geography
Grazhdansky is located 24 km (15 mi) north of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Kalinin is the nearest rural locality.

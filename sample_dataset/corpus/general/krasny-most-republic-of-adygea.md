# Krasny Most, Republic of Adygea

Krasny Most (Russian: Красный Мост; Adyghe: Лъэмыдж Плъыжь, Tləmydž Ptlyź) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Krasnooktyabrskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 40 as of 2018. There are 3 streets.

## Geography
Krasny Most is located 13 km northwest of Tulsky (the district's administrative centre) by road. Sadovy is the nearest rural locality.

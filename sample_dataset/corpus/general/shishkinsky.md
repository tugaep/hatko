# Shishkinsky

Shishkinsky (Russian: Шишкинский; Adyghe: Шишкинскэр) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Sergiyevskoye Rural Settlement of Giaginsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population of this khutor was 44 as of 2018. There are 2 streets.

## Geography
The khutor is located on the left bank of the Fars River, 38 km southeast of Giaginskaya (the district's administrative centre) by road. Yekaterinovsky is the nearest rural locality.

## Ethnicity
The khutor is inhabited by Russians.

# Krasny Fars

Krasny Fars (Russian: Красный Фарс; Adyghe: Фэрзэ Плъыжь) is a rural locality (a khutor) in Dmitriyevskoye Rural Settlement of Koshekhablsky District, Adygea, Russia. The population was 145 as of 2018. There are 5 streets.

## Geography
Krasny Fars is located 15 km west of Koshekhabl (the district's administrative centre) by road. Novoalexeyevsky is the nearest rural locality.

# Podgorny (rural locality)

Podgorny (Russian: Подгорный; Adyghe: Къушъхьэ лъачI) is a settlement in urban okrug of Maykop, Russia. The population was 773 as of 2018. There are 9 streets.

## Geography
Podgorny is located 12 km north of Maykop (the district's administrative centre) by road. Sovetsky is the nearest rural locality.

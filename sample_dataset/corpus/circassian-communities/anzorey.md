# Anzorey

Anzorey (Russian: Анзорей, Kabardian: Андзорей, romanized: Andzorey) is a rural locality (a selo) and the administrative center of Leskensky District of the Kabardino-Balkar Republic, Russia. Population:  6,551 (2010 census); 6,931 (2002 census);

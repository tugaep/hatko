# Assokolay

Assokolay (Russian: Ассоколай; Adyghe: Аскъэлай) is a rural locality (an aul) in Teuchezhsky District of the  Republic of Adygea, Russia. It is located southeast of Ponezhukay and has mostly Adyghe population.

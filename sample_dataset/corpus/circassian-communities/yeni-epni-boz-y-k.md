# Yeniçepni, Bozüyük

Yeniçepni, formerly and still commonly known as Çerkesçepni (Adyghe: Адыгэчэпни, romanized: Adıgəçəpni) is a village in the Bozüyük District, Bilecik Province, Turkey. Its population is 75 (2021). As the name of the village suggests, it is populated by ethnic Circassians from the Hatuqay tribe.

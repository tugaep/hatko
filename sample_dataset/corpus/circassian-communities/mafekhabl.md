# Mafekhabl

Mafekhabl (Russian: Мафэхабль; Adyghe: Мафэхьабл) is a rural locality (an aul) in Kirovskoye Rural Settlement of Maykopsky District, Russia. The population was 140 as of 2018. There are 7 streets. The village was founded after the Circassians of Kosovo emigrated to the Republic of Adyghea in the Russian Federation as refugees due to the war.
